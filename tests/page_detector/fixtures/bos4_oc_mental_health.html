Mental Health Resources

Orange County residents have access to various mental health resources, and it’s just as important that we take care of our mental health during this pandemic. If you are in crisis or need help, please see the resources below:

 

• National Alliance on Mental Illness - NAMI Orange County, CA Warm Line

~ (877) 910-9276 or (714) 991-6412 (call or text)

~ Available 24/7

 

• Community Helpline

~ For crisis and non-crisis calls

~ (877) 541-2525

~ Available 8 am to 8 pm daily

~Spanish-speaking counselors are available 

 

• California Peer-Run Warm Line

~ (855) 845-7415

~ Available 24/7, call or chat

 

• Seniors’ Friendship Line

~ (800) 971-0016

~ 60+ adults with disability for non-crisis and crisis calls

~ Non-emergency calls 8 am to 8 pm daily

~ Crisis calls available 24/7

~ Varied availability for Spanish-speaking counselors

 

• Northern Valley Talk Line

~ (855) 582-5554

~ Non-crisis, peer-to-peer counseling

~ 4:30 pm to 9:30 pm daily

 

• Southern Valley Warm Line

~ (866) 761-4899

~ Trained peer advocates

~ Available Mon-Fri 2 pm to 5 pm

~ Available Saturday 9 am to 5 pm

~ Sunday - Closed

 

• Didi Hirsch National Suicide Prevention Lifeline (NSPL)

~ 988

 

• Disaster Distress Helpline

~ 800-985-5990

~ 24/7 and offers language support

 

•Teen line

~ Call 800-852-8336 from 6-10pm PST

~ Text "TEEN" to 839863

 

• JED Foundation

~ For teens and young adults

~ Text "HOME" to 741-741

~ 800-273-TALK (8255)

 

• Trevor line (LGBTQ Youth)

~ 866-488-7386

~ Text: 678-678

 

• Korean Crisis line  

~ 877-727-4747

Available from 4:30 PM to 12:30 AM daily

 

• Crisis Chat 

~Crisis Text for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing – text HEARME to 839863

 

• Social Services Information – 211OC

 

• For Survivor of Suicide Loss, Bereavement Services in Orange County 

~ (714) 547-0885

 

• Stigma Free OC

 

• Be Well OC

949-749-2500
